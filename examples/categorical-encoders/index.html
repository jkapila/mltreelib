Leakage Protection in Supervised Encoders¶

Target-derived features can leak a row's outcome into its own representation. Split data first. Fit encoders only on training folds, and use out-of-fold training representations when appropriate. Fit the final encoder on the training set before transforming a held-out set. The following methods address different aspects of this problem: - CatBoostEncoder: Ordered target statistics using seeded random row permutations. For level '3' (which appears 89 times), CatBoostEncoder assigns 89 distinct incremental target statistic values on train, reducing direct reuse of the current row target. - LeaveOneOutEncoder: Subtracts the target of the current row: \((S - y_i) / (n - 1)\). - MEstimatorEncoder: Shrinks small-count categories toward the global prior, but does not by itself prevent target leakage: \((S + m \cdot p) / (n + m)\).

Weight of Evidence & Neural Embeddings¶

  • WoEEncoder: Computes \(\ln(\text{distribution of non-events} / \text{distribution of events})\) for credit risk and churn scorecards.
  • EmbeddingEncoder: Trains dense entity embeddings directly in pure Rust via stochastic gradient descent with an SGD optimizer and cosine decay schedule.

Ordered statistics and leave-one-out encoding do not replace an independent evaluation. For grouped or time-ordered data, construct folds that respect groups or time. The training accuracy above is illustrative and is not a generalization estimate.
