Hyperparameter search (Optuna)¶

The full ModelTree growth surface is a search space — and Optuna is the wheel we borrow for it. examples/hpo_optuna.py tunes depth, leaf size, ridge, leaf kind, criterion, the split grid, categorical search, the imbalance knobs and pruning on a seeded TPE study:

uv run --with pandas --with optuna python examples/hpo_optuna.py 40

The objective is plain — fit, score, return RMSE. Every suggestion maps 1:1 to a ModelTree keyword:

params = dict(
    max_depth=trial.suggest_int("max_depth", 1, 8),
    min_rows=trial.suggest_int("min_rows", 5, 200, log=True),
    l2=trial.suggest_float("l2", 1e-3, 10.0, log=True),
    leaf=trial.suggest_categorical("leaf", ["linear", "mean"]),
    leaf_cols=trial.suggest_categorical("leaf_cols", ["all", "path"]),
    gain=trial.suggest_categorical("gain", ["sse", "sdr"]),
    split_precision=trial.suggest_categorical("split_precision", [None, 0.1, 0.01]),
    cat_search=trial.suggest_categorical("cat_search", ["cumulative", "single_vs_rest", "both"]),
    alpha=trial.suggest_float("alpha", 0.0, 0.2),
    imbalance_penalty=trial.suggest_float("imbalance_penalty", 1e-3, 100.0, log=True),
    prune=trial.suggest_categorical("prune", ["none", "bic", "mdl"]),
)
m = ModelTree(**params)
m.fit(xtr, ytr, dtypes=dtypes)
return Metrics().regression(yte, m.predict(xte))["rmse"]

trial #38  (seeded TPE, airfoil 80/20)

Two findings worth keeping: TPE independently rejected the split grid (split_precision=None) for this fit, and it picked prune='bic' with a small imbalance penalty — cost pruning and balance tuning, chosen by the search rather than by hand.

The winner prints in the exact SETTINGS dict shape the Elo rounds record — paste it into examples/elo_round.py to score a tuned flavor on the full field. That is the intended loop: search per-dataset, then let the round measure what the settings are actually worth across 18 datasets (ELO — Running a round).
