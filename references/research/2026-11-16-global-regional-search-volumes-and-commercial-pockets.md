## 2. Distinct Commercial & Technical Pockets

### Pocket 1: India — Massive Volume, Developer Scale & Fintech Credit
- **Search Profile**:
  - `credit scoring`: **201,000 searches/mo**. The largest single keyword volume in the dataset. India's UPI, digital lending, NBFCs, and alternative credit risk scoring create insatiable demand for transparent tabular scoring models.
  - `decision tree` (**22,200/mo**) and `cart algorithm` (**480/mo**) exceed the US market.
  - `explainable ai`: **8,100/mo** (3.4x higher than US 2,400).
- **Go-To-Market Angle**:
  - Open-source developer adoption, GitHub stars, technical tutorials, and financial risk credit scorecards.

### Pocket 2: Germany (DACH) — Industrie 4.0 & Sensor Maintenance ($168 CPC)
- **Search Profile**:
  - `predictive maintenance`: **2,900 searches/mo** at **$167.70 CPC**.
  - `lightgbm`: **$143.74 CPC**; `prescriptive analytics`: **$122.51 CPC**.
  - `explainable ai`: **1,000 searches/mo** driven by EU AI Act compliance.
- **Go-To-Market Angle**:
  - Position `mltreelib`'s lag/seasonal transforms (`timefeat`) and rule-based models for industrial IoT, sensor failure prediction, and explainable automotive/engineering diagnostics.

### Pocket 3: United Kingdom — London Financial Services & Fraud ($229 CPC)
- **Search Profile**:
  - `fraud detection`: **$228.63 CPC**.
  - High demand for auditable banking risk models compliant with PRA/FCA regulations.
- **Go-To-Market Angle**:
  - High-skew fraud classification and tree auditability (C5.0/Cubist rule extraction).

### Pocket 4: Middle East (UAE / Gulf) — Energy & Supply Chain ($104 CPC)
- **Search Profile**:
  - UAE: `predictive maintenance` (**$103.54 CPC**), `demand forecasting` (**$17.10 CPC**).
  - Backed by oil & gas (ADNOC/Aramco infrastructure) and Dubai logistics hubs.
- **Go-To-Market Angle**:
  - Longitudinal multi-step forecasting and asset integrity monitoring.

### Pocket 5: Australia — Resource & Enterprise SaaS
- **Search Profile**:
  - Steady adoption across `decision tree` (2,900), `xgboost` (1,300), and `tabular data` (320).
